Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24 September 2026

Introduction

Smart Calculator ("we", "our", "app") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Information We Collect

Smart Calculator does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information. All calculations, notes, and photos are stored locally on your device.

Private Notes Locker

The Private Notes feature stores your notes locally on your device. No data is sent to any server. You can delete all notes at any time by resetting your PIN.

Photo Locker

The Photo Locker feature allows you to hide photos in your app's private storage. Photos are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to any server. Photos can be deleted anytime from within the app.

Biometric Authentication

The app may use your device's biometric authentication (fingerprint or face) to unlock the Private Locker. Biometric data is processed by your device's operating system and is never accessed or stored by the app.

PDF Generation

PDF statements are generated locally on your device. These files are only saved to your device when you choose to download or share them.

Currency Conversion Data

The currency converter fetches live exchange rates from public APIs (Frankfurter - European Central Bank). No personal data is sent during this process.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

  • Internet: For fetching live currency exchange rates only
  • Biometric: For unlocking the Private Locker
  • Photos/Media: For the Photo Locker feature

Third-Party Services

We use the Frankfurter API (European Central Bank data) for currency rates. Their privacy policy applies to that service.

Data Security

All your data (notes, photos) is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. This storage is not accessible to other apps. We recommend keeping your device secure with a screen lock.

Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
